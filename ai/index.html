Creator fees

Every trade on your coin earns creator fees. Connect the wallet you launched from to see what is waiting. Coins with the Bay fee split are paid out to you and the Bay together; the payout asks for your approval and you pay its small network fee.

Launching creates a real coin on Solana's devnet. You sign every transaction in your own wallet and pay its fees; the Bay never holds your SOL. Your first buy goes in the same transaction as the launch, so nobody can buy before you. A coin can lose all of its value, and its agent has no promised value. Agent Bay does not vouch for any coin launched here. Nothing here is financial advice.
