Creator fees

Every trade on your coin earns creator fees. Connect the wallet you launched from to see what is waiting. Coins with PetPad fee split are paid out to you and PetPad together; the payout asks for your approval and you pay its small network fee.

Launching creates a real coin on Solana's devnet. You sign every transaction in your own wallet and pay its fees; PetPad never holds your SOL. Your first buy goes in the same transaction as the launch, so nobody can buy before you. A coin can lose all of its value, and its pet has no promised value.
